Xiaona Wang currently works as a postdoctoral fellow in the Faculty of Education at East China Normal University (ECNU), where her research addresses the geopolitics of higher education, academic mobility and international scientific collaboration. Before that, she served for one year as a Doctoral Teaching Fellow in the Department of Education, University of Oxford, teaching on postgraduate-level modules. She was also a Research Assistant at the Centre for Global Higher Education (CGHE) and has worked at the Global Education Cluster at UNICEF in Geneva.

She holds a DPhil from the Department of Education, University of Oxford, where her studies were fully funded by a Clarendon Scholarship. She is also a recipient of the Dahrendorf Scholarship. Before her DPhil, Xiaona obtained an MA at Peking University and a BA at Beijing Normal University in China, graduating with first-class honours.

Her research expertise focuses on collaboration studies, research on research, and the influence of geopolitics in higher education within STEMM disciplines. Her doctoral thesis employs a mixed-methods design to investigate the dynamics of international scientific collaboration between China and Switzerland.
